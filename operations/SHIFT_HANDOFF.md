# Shift Handoff

The lab is not staffed in shifts. This template demonstrates the context a 24x7 SOC platform team should transfer without relying on verbal memory.

~~~text
Handoff time (UTC):
Outgoing / incoming role:
Platform health: healthy | degraded
Open SEV-1/SEV-2 items:
Failed or paused playbooks:
Alerts awaiting replay:
Cases awaiting analyst action:
Response proposals awaiting decision:
Endpoint last-seen concerns:
Changes in progress / rollback point:
Capacity or certificate concerns:
Next action, owner, due time:
Evidence and runbook links:
~~~

The incoming operator acknowledges the handoff, runs the health snapshot, and accepts ownership of timed actions. Never include credentials, complete payloads, real personal data, or unrestricted forensic content.
